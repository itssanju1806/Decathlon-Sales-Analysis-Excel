## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents a comprehensive **Sales & Customer Analysis for Decathlon**, developed entirely in **Microsoft Excel**.

The analysis focuses on evaluating business performance across multiple dimensions, including:

- Sales and revenue performance
- Year-over-Year (YoY) growth
- Order and customer trends
- Average Order Value (AOV)
- Sports-wise performance
- Product category contribution
- Customer retention
- Gender-wise sales contribution
- Monthly sales trends

The objective is to transform raw sales data into **meaningful business insights and actionable recommendations** that can support revenue growth, customer retention, and better category-level decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

Decathlon operates across multiple sports and product categories, making it important to understand **what drives revenue, order volume, and customer value**.

This analysis aims to answer the following business questions:

- How is overall sales performance changing YoY?
- Is revenue growth being driven by customers, orders, or AOV?
- Which sports generate the highest sales and order volume?
- Which product categories contribute the most to revenue?
- How does sales performance vary across months?
- What is the current customer retention rate?
- How is sales contribution distributed across genders?
- Which areas provide the greatest opportunities for revenue and customer growth?

# 🔍 Key Insights

### 1. 📈 Strong YoY Business Growth

- Sales increased by **40.8% YoY**.
- Orders increased by **40.1% YoY**.
- Customer base increased by **13.5% YoY**.
- The business demonstrates strong overall growth and increasing transaction activity.

### 2. 💰 Revenue Growth is Primarily Volume-Driven

- AOV increased by only **0.5% YoY**.
- Sales and order growth are significantly higher than AOV growth.
- This indicates that revenue growth is primarily driven by **higher order volumes rather than increased spending per order**.

### 3. 🚴 Cycling is the Leading Sports Segment

- Cycling generated approximately **₹8.1 Cr in sales**.
- It is the strongest revenue-generating sports segment.
- Continued focus on Cycling can help sustain category-level revenue growth.

### 4. 🏕️ Outdoor Shows Strong Performance

- Outdoor generated approximately **₹4.1 Cr in sales**.
- The segment demonstrates strong customer demand and provides opportunities for category expansion and cross-selling.

### 5. 🏃 Running Leads in Order Volume

- Running recorded approximately **3,151 orders**.
- Despite its high transaction volume, its revenue contribution is lower than Cycling.
- This indicates an opportunity to increase **revenue per order** through premium products and complementary product sales.

### 6. 🔄 Healthy Customer Retention

- Customer retention stands at approximately **64.58%**.
- A healthy retention rate provides a strong foundation for repeat purchases and long-term customer value.

### 7. 👥 Balanced Gender Contribution

- Male and female customers each contribute approximately **50% of total sales**.
- This indicates a balanced customer base without significant dependence on one gender segment.

### 8. 📅 Consistent Monthly Sales Performance

- Monthly sales remain relatively stable with moderate fluctuations.
- These patterns provide opportunities for better inventory planning and targeted promotional activities.

### 9. 🛍️ Product Category Opportunities

- Product categories contribute differently to overall sales.
- High-performing categories can be prioritized for inventory and marketing investment.
- Lower-performing categories can be reviewed for potential optimization.

# 💡 Business Recommendations

The recommendations below are directly linked to the analysis and dashboard findings.

### 💳 Increase Average Order Value

Since AOV grew by only **0.5% YoY**:

- Use cross-selling and upselling strategies.
- Promote complementary products.
- Introduce sport-specific product bundles.
- Encourage premium product purchases.

### 🚴 Strengthen Cycling Performance

With Cycling contributing approximately **₹8.1 Cr**:

- Maintain strong inventory availability.
- Develop targeted Cycling promotions.
- Expand complementary Cycling products.
- Encourage bundled purchases.

### 🏃 Improve Running Monetization

Running has the highest order volume among the major sports.

Focus on:

- Premium Running products
- Accessories and complementary products
- Product bundles
- Relevant product recommendations

### 🏕️ Build on Outdoor Performance

With approximately **₹4.1 Cr in sales**:

- Expand the Outdoor category.
- Promote complementary products.
- Leverage seasonal demand.
- Develop targeted Outdoor campaigns.

### 🔄 Strengthen Customer Retention

With retention at **64.58%**:

- Strengthen loyalty initiatives.
- Personalize repeat-purchase campaigns.
- Target existing customers with relevant offers.
- Encourage higher purchase frequency.

### 👤 Convert Customer Growth into Higher Value

Customer growth stands at **13.5% YoY**, while AOV growth remains low.

The focus should be on converting:

**New Customers → Repeat Customers → High-Value Customers**

### 📅 Leverage Monthly Sales Trends

Monthly sales patterns can support:

- Inventory planning
- Promotional scheduling
- Seasonal campaigns
- Demand-based resource allocation
